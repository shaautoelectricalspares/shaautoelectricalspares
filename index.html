How to Order

A simple order process for online customers.

  1. Choose the product and note its SKU.
  2. Send the SKU, quantity and delivery PIN code.
  3. For vehicle-specific parts, share make, model, year and a photo of the old part.
  4. Confirm stock, final price and shipping charge.
  5. Pay through the agreed traceable payment method and keep the order confirmation.
